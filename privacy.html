Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Matthieu Delatour ("we", "us") handles information when you use ReFoot (the "Service"), a football highlights aggregator that shows fixtures, results, standings, and links to match highlights hosted on third-party platforms.

1. Information we collect

Account information

ReFoot works without an account. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account identifier so your on-device experience can function. If you choose to sign in with Google, we link your Google account to that identifier and receive from Google your email address, display name, and profile photo. We use these only to show which account you are signed in with in the app's menu; we do not copy them into our database. Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. We do not receive your Google password.

Your preferences and favourites

Your favourite teams and competitions, and app preferences such as theme and team colours, are stored on your device.

If you use ReFoot without signing in, they stay on your device only.

If you sign in with Google, we also save your favourites and these preferences to your account in our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore), so they are available on any device where you sign in. The first time you sign in, favourites already on your device are combined with any already saved to your account, so none are lost; if your account already has saved preferences (such as theme), those are used. Only you can read or change the data saved to your account. A few device-only settings, such as whether you dismissed an in-app tip, are never synced.

If you upload a picture for the in-app assistant, it is resized and stored on your device only. It is never sent to us, uploaded, or synced to your account.

Highlight playback

Highlights are not hosted by ReFoot. When you choose to watch a highlight, ReFoot opens it on a third-party platform (currently YouTube), in your browser or the YouTube app. That platform may set its own cookies and receive technical information such as your IP address and device details, governed by that platform's own privacy policy. See "Third-party services" below.

Technical and usage information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request logs) to deliver and secure the Service.

We do not currently collect usage analytics about how you interact with the Service. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe what is collected and your choices before that feature is enabled.

ReFoot reads football data (fixtures, results, standings) and highlight links from a cache we prepare in advance. The app does not send your personal information to the football data sources or to the highlight platforms; opening a highlight you choose to watch takes you to that platform.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service, including sign-in and syncing your favourites and preferences.
  • To remember your settings across sessions and devices.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the basis of: performance of a contract (providing the Service you request), our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service), your consent (where required, e.g. for optional analytics), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

We rely on the following third parties, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google / Firebase: authentication, cloud database, and hosting.
  • YouTube (Google): highlight videos, opened on YouTube.
  • Wikimedia Commons: when the in-app assistant shows a football legend's profile, their freely licensed photo is loaded from Wikimedia's servers (upload.wikimedia.org), which receive standard technical information such as your IP address. "Read on Wikipedia" opens Wikipedia in your browser.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. Please review their policies, including Google's Privacy Policy, for how they handle your information.

5. Data retention

We retain account and preference data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we immediately and permanently delete your sign-in account and the favourites and preferences saved to it, and clear that data from the device you deleted it on. Copies stored on other devices where you used ReFoot may stay on those devices until you remove them there (for example by clearing the browser's site data). Technical information processed by our hosting providers (see "Technical and usage information") is kept under those providers' own retention schedules. If you ask us by email instead, we delete or de-identify the associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • Sign out at any time from the app menu. Signing out keeps your account.
  • Remove favourites and change preferences in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: open the menu and tap Delete account under your name, then confirm with Google. This permanently deletes your account and the favourites and preferences saved to it, and can't be undone.
  • Or request account and data deletion by contacting us at refoot.highlights.app@gmail.com.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by California law. You may exercise your rights by contacting us at the address above.

7. Children's privacy

ReFoot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We and our providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Material changes tied to new data practices will be reflected here when those practices launch.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: refoot.highlights.app@gmail.com.
