Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Matthieu Delatour ("we", "us") handles information when you use ReFoot (the "Service"), a football highlights aggregator that shows fixtures, results, standings, and links to match highlights hosted on third-party platforms.

1. Information we collect

Account information

ReFoot works without an account. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account identifier so your on-device experience can function. If you choose to sign in with Google, we link your Google account to that identifier and receive from Google your email address, display name, and profile photo. We do not receive your Google password.

Your preferences and favourites

Your favourite teams and competitions, and app preferences such as theme, are stored on your device. They are not currently sent to or stored on our servers. If we later add cross-device syncing of these preferences, we will update this policy to describe it before that feature launches.

Highlight playback

Highlights are not hosted by ReFoot. When you play a highlight, the video is embedded from a third-party platform (currently YouTube). That platform may set its own cookies and receive technical information such as your IP address and device details, governed by that platform's own privacy policy. See "Third-party services" below.

Technical and usage information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request logs) to deliver and secure the Service.

We do not currently collect usage analytics about how you interact with the Service. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe what is collected and your choices before that feature is enabled.

ReFoot reads football data (fixtures, results, standings) and highlight links from a cache we prepare in advance. The app does not send your personal information to the football data sources or to the highlight platforms except as part of loading an embedded video you choose to play.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service, including sign-in and syncing your favourites and preferences.
  • To remember your settings across sessions and devices.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the basis of: performance of a contract (providing the Service you request), our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service), your consent (where required, e.g. for optional analytics), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

We rely on the following third parties, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google / Firebase: authentication, cloud database, and hosting.
  • YouTube (Google): embedded highlight playback.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. Please review their policies, including Google's Privacy Policy, for how they handle your information.

5. Data retention

We retain account and preference data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete or de-identify the associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • Sign out at any time from the app menu.
  • Remove favourites and change preferences in the app.
  • Request account and data deletion by contacting us at refoot.highlights.app@gmail.com.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by California law. You may exercise your rights by contacting us at the address above.

7. Children's privacy

ReFoot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We and our providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Material changes tied to new data practices will be reflected here when those practices launch.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: refoot.highlights.app@gmail.com.
